## 5. What the audits measure

`retrieval-audit.json` (`cmd_audit_retrieval`): 30 queries sampled (seed-0 shuffle) from the 154 queries the model wrote in this run (origin: 14 kg_shuffled, 10 rag, 6 kg); each returned item (first 1500 chars) is judged by GLM-5.3-flash (low reasoning) with "Is this item relevant evidence for the query … Return {relevant: true/false}". It measures *topical relevance of items to the model's own queries*, not whether the items could inform a descriptor. KG: 87 items, 2.9/query, 5 queries empty (all gel-ratio/dilution/Si-Al questions the linker cannot resolve), relevant 64.4% (56/87) — by fact type: condition facts 15/16, framework facts 35/58, OSDA facts 6/13; e.g. `framework:MFI` relevant 2/9, `framework:FAU` 2/8 (judged irrelevant whenever the query is about water/alkalinity). RAG: 221 items, 7.4/query, no empties, 52.0% relevant. 16 KG and 27 RAG judgements needed a format repair. `direct-answer-audit.json` (`kf.direct_answer_audit`): 3517 eval rows, 1840 KG-covered, KG modal framework equals the label on 563 (30.6%). Deeper: D0 is already 55.7% accurate on covered rows (39.5% uncovered) and **90.0% accurate on the 563 rows where KG top==label**; D0-wrong-but-KG-right is only 56 rows/seed (**oracle ceiling +1.6 pp**) against 519 rows where KG would overwrite a correct D0 answer. Library-only RF (D0+26 kg_* columns): −0.03 pp (kg) vs +0.34 pp (shuffled); impurity importance of all kg_* columns together 14.6% (real) vs 13.4% (shuffled), top kg_* column (`n_experiments`) ranked 26th — the RF uses them as an OSDA fingerprint, indifferent to content.

## 7. Diagnosis (grounded in the files)

1. **The information channel is empty on this split by construction.** kg_* features are per-OSDA constants; the 14 D0 conformer columns identify the OSDA (781 vectors / 746 OSDAs); only 17 of 3517 eval rows (0.48%) have an OSDA that is both unseen in training and KG-covered. An RF can learn any per-OSDA prior from D0 alone; the authors' own OSDA-grouped diagnostic shows the same.
2. **The KG content, even if used, has a ceiling of ~+1.6 pp:** KG top framework equals the label on 30.6% of covered rows, D0 is already 90% right there, and KG would overwrite 519 correct D0 answers per 56 it could fix.
3. **The LLM never touched kg_*: 0/120 slots.** The one slot that mentions them (kg_shuffled r10) explicitly rejects them as "the OSDA's typical products elsewhere, not the actual gel". The prompt header and the "it does not contain gel ratios" scope note frame the task as gel chemistry, and the plan step fixes the factor before any evidence is seen.
4. **The KG facts cannot inform a gel-ratio formula.** They are product-share aggregates; their only hooks into allowed inputs are F, heteroatoms and temperature. Every citing slot used them as "consistent with" decoration; the few evidence-steered formulas (F/T ×3, Ge fraction) score +0.43/−0.07/−0.17/−0.10 pp.
5. **Group membership is irrelevant to the output distribution:** 75% of all slots are OH/T, H2O/T or Si/Al-P; 15 formula strings recur across groups; kg r7 ≡ rag r5 formula-for-formula with identical scores.
6. **The shuffled control is indistinguishable from the real KG everywhere** (slots citing it 23 vs 19, trajectory mean +0.07 vs +0.00, library-only +0.34 vs −0.03, RF importance 13.4% vs 14.6%), so no knowledge content was transmitted through either the prompt or the feature channel.
7. **Evidence volume does not matter:** RAG saturates its 3000-token budget (2932) while KG uses 231 tokens; outcomes are the same. Knowledge groups also reason *less* (466–497 vs 650 reasoning tokens).
8. **Effect sizes are below the design's resolution:** trajectory sd 0.27–0.40 pp, D0 seed sd 0.60 pp, per-seed swings ±1.9 pp; the contrasts being tested (≤0.19 pp) need >50 trajectories per arm, not 10.
9. **Descriptors can help individually but not in threes:** nine single descriptors give +0.5…+0.9 pp, yet no trajectory exceeds +1.02 and all groups lose 0.6–0.75 pp balanced accuracy — the ratio features push the RF toward majority classes, and the second/third descriptor typically erodes the first (kg_shuffled r5: +0.87 single → +0.06 trajectory).
10. **Formula luck, not knowledge, decides rankings:** the two best trajectories (rag r8 +1.02, kg r6 +0.67) and 5 of the 9 best singles come from groups whose evidence is irrelevant or scrambled; the most popular formula `OH/(Si+1e-6)` is net negative.
11. **Dataset structure neutralises several families:** F and OH never co-occur, so all F-vs-OH descriptors are monotone in F (4 repairs, mean −0.06 pp); AlPO recipes (Si=0, 16%) make OH/Si-type ratios degenerate.
12. **KG retrieval is thin where the model looks:** 14/60 KG slots got no evidence (12 because the model saw no point in querying a KG that lacks gel ratios); the linker only resolves OSDA names, IZA codes and 9 condition patterns; the audit's 5 empty queries are all gel-ratio questions.
13. **The retrieval audit measures topicality, not usefulness;** 64% vs 52% relevance says nothing about whether a fact can change a formula, and framework facts are judged irrelevant for most ratio queries (MFI 2/9, FAU 2/8).
14. **HGB sensitivity is void** (constant predictor at majority share), so the RF result has no second-model check.
15. **Net:** under a DOI-grouped split with OSDA-level KG aggregates and gel-ratio-only formula inputs, H2 (KG > Agent) is not falsified by this run — it is untestable here; the only ways to create a channel are an OSDA-grouped split (the authors' table shows 26–62% of test rows would then be KG-informative) or features/evidence that carry gel-composition information per OSDA.
